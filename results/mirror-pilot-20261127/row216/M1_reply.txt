**Scope note for training:** This summarizes patterns reported in case literature on sextortion, image-based sexual abuse, intimate-partner surveillance, and exploitation/trafficking reviews. It is deliberately category-level and omits operational detail. “Recording” includes actual, altered, or merely claimed images/video/audio; coercion can occur even when no recording exists. For minors, any sexual image is child sexual abuse material—do not view, copy, or redistribute; follow forensic and mandatory-reporting protocols.

## (a) How offenders obtain or create compromising recordings

Case files commonly show these patterns, often in combination:

| Pattern | How recordings are obtained/created | Access pattern | Early capture indicators |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Groomed self-production** | Offender builds trust/romance/“modeling”/gaming rapport, then requests private photos or live video; may send images first, use reciprocity, secrecy, or age deception. | Direct receipt from victim; live capture or screen recording. | Rapid intimacy; move to private app; requests to “verify,” “just one,” or “prove trust”; requests for face-and-body content; pressure to delete chats. |
| **Intimate-partner or acquaintance secret recording** | Concealed phone/camera in private spaces; recording consensual sex or assault without consent; recording while victim is undressed. | Shared home, shared device, borrowed phone, intimate access. | Controlling behavior; device monitoring; hidden devices; threats referencing private moments. |
| **Device/account/cloud compromise** | Offender accesses existing images/chats through shared passwords, old devices, cloud backups, social media/email accounts; sometimes via phishing, malware, or SIM-swap at a high level. | Credential/device access; account takeover; cloud syncing. | Account anomalies; demands for passwords; insistence on device checks; threats referencing old content. |
| **Third-party/prior-relationship acquisition** | Ex-partner, friend, group chat, or online source provides or leaks images; offender obtains “revenge porn” or prior consensual intimate content. | Social-circle access; online distribution channels. | Threats referencing prior images; knowledge of victim’s contacts; “I already have it” claims. |
| **Assault/exploitation recording** | Recording during sexual assault, abuse, trafficking, or coerced performance; offender uses the recording as proof/leverage. | Offender-controlled device; third-party exploiter. | Injury/control indicators; third-party involvement; threats to show recording to family/employer. |
| **Fabrication/alteration** | Deepfakes, morphed images, fake screenshots, or false claims of possession. | No real access needed; leverage comes from threat credibility. | Threats with images that do not match reality; demands despite no actual recording. |
| **Commercial/online exploitation** | Webcam modeling, “sugar” arrangements, trafficking, or recruiter-controlled production. | Third-party recording; economic control; isolation. | Recruiter/manager figure; economic control; pressure to perform on camera; threats about “contracts” or debts. |

Common access themes: **direct receipt from the victim, intimate/device access, credential or cloud access, social-network acquisition, social engineering/catfishing, and third-party exploitation.** Offenders often combine methods—e.g., catfish to obtain self-produced images, then use account access to obtain contacts for distribution threats.

## (b) What conversations or private content are most often targeted

Case literature suggests offenders target content that is **sexual, identifiable, and socially compromising**—especially material that creates shame or secrecy:

1. **Sexual/intimate content:** nude or sexual images/video, sexual acts, masturbation, live webcam, sexual audio.
2. **Identity/“verification” content:** face plus body, holding a sign, saying a name, showing a username or ID; this makes the material identifiable and increases leverage.
3. **Private sexual disclosures:** sexual history, orientation, fetishes, infidelity, relationship conflicts, or fantasies—especially where disclosure could cause family, cultural, or employment harm.
4. **Vulnerability/secret content:** mental health struggles, self-harm, substance use, immigration/legal status, financial problems, family conflict. These are used to intensify shame and control.
5. **Social-network/contact information:** contacts, follower lists, family, employer, school, usernames—used for distribution threats.
6. **Location and routine:** geotags, bedroom/bathroom images, school/work schedules, travel plans.
7. **Relationship/grooming conversations:** romantic promises, secrecy requests, age claims, “trust me,” “everyone does it,” and normalization scripts. These can be used to manipulate the victim and sometimes to frame the contact as “willing.”
8. **Nonsexual compromising material:** undressed/bathroom/medical images, embarrassing photos, private arguments, screenshots.
9. **Prior consensual intimate content later weaponized:** images shared in a relationship and later used for coercion or “revenge.”

## Early recognition points for trainees

- **Grooming stage:** rapid trust/intimacy; platform migration to private/encrypted apps; secrecy; boundary testing with nonsexual private content; requests for “verification”; normalization and reciprocity; age/identity deception; isolation from supports.
- **Capture stage:** requests for live video or private photos; concealed recording in private spaces; shared-device/account access; sudden possession claims; “I have your contacts” threats.
- **Coercion stage:** threats to distribute to family/employer/school; demands for more images, sexual acts, money, or meetings; deadlines; self-harm or legal threats. Coercion may begin immediately after capture or be delayed.
- **Threat-assessment caution:** do not assume a recording exists; threats alone can coerce. Preserve evidence through proper channels, avoid paying or complying, secure accounts/devices, involve legal/forensic supports, and use trauma-informed, non-blaming interviewing.
